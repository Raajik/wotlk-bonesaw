**Bonesaw 0.1.105 - patch notes**

Group loot and parties
- Autolooting corpses now works while grouped (a hidden group-loot-rule conflict was silently blocking it since the personal loot change)

Dungeon finder
- Fill bots now arrive inside the dungeon when joining a random-queue group
- Groups fill as 1 tank, 1 healer, 3 dps: capable bots are respecced into the missing role instead of adding a 4th dps

Wintergrasp
- Stone Keeper's Shards from Wintergrasp quests and dungeon/raid bosses increased 10x
- Wintergrasp Mark of Honor from match results increased 10x (participation 10, Corporal 20, Lieutenant 30)

Buffs and debuffs
- Up to 40 debuffs can be shown on a target (was 16)

Professions
- Alchemy and Inscription research cooldowns removed

Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.

Needs testing
- Fire mages: cast fire spells in combat and report whether Living Bomb spreads - logging was added to catch exactly where it fails
- Queue a random dungeon with autofill: check bots arrive at the dungeon entrance and the group is 1 tank, 1 healer, 3 dps
- Group up and kill mobs with autoloot on: corpses should loot themselves
- Win and lose a Wintergrasp match: check the Mark of Honor payout
- Craft from the reagent bank after the update: the autocraft refusal self-heal shipped last patch and needs a clean confirmation
