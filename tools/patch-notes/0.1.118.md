**Bonesaw 0.1.118 - patch notes**

**Changes**

**Icecrown Citadel**
- Gunship cannon shots deal far more damage on both factions, so the gunship battle ends quickly.
- The entrance speech before Deathbringer Saurfang no longer holds the door for a minute; the fight starts within a few seconds.
- Valithria Dreamwalker is restored to full health five seconds into combat, which completes the fight (bots were never going to heal her).
- The Crimson Hall door before the Blood Council is gone.

**Tanking and threat**
- Non-tank players generate 99% less threat, so burst damage and big heals stop pulling bosses off the tank. Tank perks keep their extra threat.

**The world**
- Flying mounts work in Dalaran again.
- Wintergrasp workshops capture at the same boosted speed as every other objective instead of crawling at one point per second.

**Please test these things**
- Try a flying mount inside Dalaran - you should get airborne instead of getting "You can't use that here."
- Run the gunship battle in Icecrown Citadel - cannon shots should hit much harder and the fight should end fast.
- Pull Deathbringer Saurfang - the speech should last only a few seconds before the fight starts.
- As a non-tank in a raid, land your biggest hit or heal - you should not pull the boss off the tank.
- Craft something with Blacksmithing or Leatherworking - does your crafting-speed perk actually speed it up?
- Cast Living Bomb on a group of mobs - does it spread to nearby enemies on its own?
- As a protection paladin, cast Avenger's Shield - is the cooldown short now instead of 30 seconds?
- Craft a bandage straight from the reagent bank - does it still claim you are missing materials?
