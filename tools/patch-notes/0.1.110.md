**Bonesaw 0.1.110 - patch notes**

Currencies
- Stone Keeper's Shards, Emblems, and marks now pool into a shared account balance: earn them on any character, spend them on any character. Vendor gear pays from the pool automatically.
- Avenger's Shield's cooldown is fixed and no longer gets stuck at the old 30 seconds.

Classes
- Living Bomb now spreads properly for mages with the top rank of the spell.
- Protection paladins: Thorns hits for 20x damage and is kept active on your whole party and raid.
- All tank specs (protection warrior, protection paladin, blood death knight, feral druid in Bear form) generate 100x threat -- hold aggro on anything.
- Crafting any profession is now instant with the crafting perk.

Travel and quality of life
- Exploring a new zone now unlocks every flight path on that map for all your characters.
- The movement speed cap no longer applies while flying.
- Reagent bank counts now update for every character on your account the moment one of them crafts.
- The Bonesaw launcher has a new icon: a very bloody sawblade.

Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.

Needs testing
- Try spending pooled currency (shards, emblems) on a different character than the one that earned it.
- On an alt, check that flight paths your main explored are already unlocked.
- Protection paladins: check Thorns damage and that the whole raid keeps the buff.
- As a tank, pull a big pack and see if anything peels off you.
- Fire mages: cast Living Bomb and a fire spell, then watch whether it jumps between enemies.
