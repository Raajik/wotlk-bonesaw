**Bonesaw 0.1.94 - patch notes**

**Dungeon Finder**
- Fixed the red Lua errors that appeared every time a dungeon group was found. The ready popup now fills all five seats correctly instead of leaving unreadable slots behind.
- Group members show the right role icon, and the leader crown is back on the ready window.

**Class perks**
- Affliction is properly contagious now. Every infected enemy spreads your damage-over-time effects to enemies near it each second, so the plague creeps outward on its own instead of only hitting a ring around your current target.
- The spread keeps going when you switch targets or your first victim dies.

**Account perks**
- Fixed items attuned automatically on loot being worth a fraction of the same item attuned by hand. Both routes now bank the item's full value.
- Attunements that were short-changed by the old behaviour are repaired automatically the next time you log in.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
