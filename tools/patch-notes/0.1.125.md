**Bonesaw 0.1.125 - patch notes**

**Wintergrasp**
- Flying mounts now work in Wintergrasp when no battle is running. During an active battle the zone stays grounded like before.
- The champions ringing the battlefield now spawn in the full 5x amount, spread properly around the ring instead of stacked in one spot.

**Crafting and the reagent bank**
- Recipes in craft windows like First Aid can use materials from your reagent bank again. It previously only worked for recipes sent with full item details, so bandage crafts refused to pay from the bank.

**Class fixes**
- Avenger's Shield now shows a clean 6 second cooldown. The icon was being re-locked by a stale 30 second entry right after the server cleared it - the clear now lands after the cast settles.

**Attuning**
- Auto-attune's item level filter is now a maximum, not a minimum: set a cap and auto-attune only consumes items at or below it, so your upgrades are safe. The button reads Max ilvl.

**Bots**
- Playerbot Death Knights no longer channel Army of the Dead on their own - the interrupted cast never triggered its cooldown, so they looped ghouls endlessly.

**Please test these things**
- On a flying mount, fly around Wintergrasp while no battle is running - then check you still stay grounded while a battle is on.
- Learn a First Aid recipe like Heavy Frostweave Bandage, move the cloth out of your bags, and craft from the profession window - it should pay from the reagent bank.
- Cast Avenger's Shield a few times and watch the icon - it should show a 6 second cooldown, not 30.
- Set a Max ilvl for auto-attune on the armory page and loot something better than the cap - auto-attune should leave the upgrade alone.
