**Bonesaw 0.1.123 - patch notes**

Crafting
- Crafting now works from everywhere, not just the profession window. Clicking a craft spell in your spellbook, on a bar button, or in a macro will pay reagents straight out of your reagent bank - the client used to refuse to even try when your bags looked empty, so vault-funded crafts were impossible outside the profession window.
- Crafts your bags can already pay for behave exactly as before. Nothing is ever withdrawn into your bags - the vault pays in place, and the finished item lands in your bags like any other craft.
