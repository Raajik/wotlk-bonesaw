**Bonesaw 0.1.84 - patch notes**

**One Items tab**
- Gear, Attune and the Armory are now a single Items tab. Everything you own is in one list.
- Each row shows the item, its level, and exactly what levelling has added to it so far.
- A state column tells you whether each item is worn, sitting in your bags, or attuned and waiting to be created.
- Attuned items you have not created yet have a Create button right on the row. The Armory is no longer somewhere you navigate to.
- Filter by Armor, Weapons or Attuned only, and search by name.

**Item stats you could not see before**
- Last version items started growing crit, hit, haste, attack power, spell power and weapon damage, but the panel could only ever show a third of that. It now shows all of it.
- Items also show a proper icon and tooltip.

**Perks panel**
- Ranks you earn by playing now say what earns them, instead of just "Earned by playing". You will see things like "Win a battleground" or "Have 2 characters at level 80".

**Removed**
- The World tab is gone. Everything it did now lives in the Unlocks and Perks tabs on the achievement window.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
