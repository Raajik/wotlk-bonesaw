**Bonesaw 0.1.80 - patch notes**

**Account Perks**
- Curator is now four ranks instead of one. Items in your bags and bank count for 25%, 50%, 75% or 100% of their value toward your account stats - straight away, no waiting.
- Gear you actually wear still grows past that by levelling, so there is never a reason to leave something in the bank instead of using it.
- Gathering perks are split into separate Yield and Reach rows, so you can see which upgrade you are actually buying.
- The perks list scrolls now instead of being crammed into one screen.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
