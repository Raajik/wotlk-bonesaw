**Bonesaw 0.1.126 - patch notes**

**Boss fixes**
- Deathbringer Saurfang now fights properly again: he walks to his position, the door closes behind him, and only then does he engage. Attacking him during the entrance no longer makes him run back through the door and sit there unattackable.
- Archavon the Stone Watcher and Gormok the Impaler were silently dealing ten times the damage their combat log showed, because a damage multiplier meant only for real siege engines was landing on any boss that carries a vehicle seat in its data. That multiplier now only applies to actual siege engines (Wintergrasp, Strand of the Ancients, Isle of Conquest and Ulduar's vehicle fights).

**Please test these things**
- Pull Deathbringer Saurfang in Icecrown Citadel: he should fight you on his platform with the door closed behind him, and attacking him early should not send him back through the door.
- Give Archavon in Vault of Archavon and Gormok in Trial of the Crusader a try: they should hit for what the combat log shows instead of one-shotting the raid.
- Try casting Haunt while moving - it should be instant now.
- Kill things in a group: every group member should be able to loot every corpse.
- Living Bomb should now jump from enemy to enemy instead of only spreading from you.
- Queue for a dungeon and check the group finder builds 1 tank / 1 healer / 3 dps again.

This update is server-side only - you do not need to update your client.
