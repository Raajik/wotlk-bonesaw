**Bonesaw 0.1.79 - patch notes**

**Account Perks**
- Achievements now pay for perks. Every achievement is worth a skill point, spent on the quality-of-life tracks in the Account Perks panel.
- New **Perks** tab on the achievement window, next to Statistics - spend your points where you earn them.
- All seven mobile summons (mailbox, bank, auction, trainer, stable, hearthstone, flight master) cost 1 point each, so a new character can grab the lot straight away.
- Refund everything and re-spend at any time, with a 5 minute cooldown. Perks you earned by playing are never taken back.
- Honor, Reputation, Factions, Leveling and Profession bonuses stay earned by playing rather than bought, and the panel shows them in their own colour.
- Buy a rank and later earn it the hard way? Your points come back automatically.

**Achievements**
- Achievements are now account-wide - earn one and every character on your account has it.
- Titles from achievements follow to your alts.
- Realm First achievements are worth ten times a normal one.

**Movement**
- Wayfarer is a straight speed track now: five ranks of 20%, up to +100%, applied equally on foot, mounted and flying. No more splitting it against damage, and no more halved mounted speed.

**Wintergrasp**
- The queue button in the battlegrounds window works. No more .wg
- Queueing during a battle drops you straight in if there is room.
- Siege vehicles move four times as fast.

**Fixes**
- Fishing pools now pay your fishing yield bonus.
- Gathering nodes auto-loot when you click them.
- Mining and herbalism yield and reach are now separate upgrades - you no longer have to max yield before buying any reach.
- Dark Iron scraps, darkmoon cards, Zul'Gurub bijous and coins, Relics of Ulduar and Argent Dawn tokens all go to the reagent bank.
- The account reputation bonus now applies to battleground factions.
- Bladestorm actually costs no rage.
- Fixed the error when equipping a bind-on-equip item.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
