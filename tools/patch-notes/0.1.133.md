**Bonesaw 0.1.133 - patch notes**

This one is a maintenance release. There are no gameplay changes in it, and
nothing you need to download - the realm just restarted onto it.

**Deathbringer Saurfang**
- Saurfang has now been reported three times for dropping combat and going
  untargetable the moment he is pulled, and two attempted fixes have not
  stopped it. The reason is that the fight was not recording enough about
  itself: every theory we fixed turned out not to match what the logs showed
  afterwards.
- This build makes the encounter report exactly what it is doing at every
  point where he could become untargetable - the walk in, the door, the
  handover into the fight, and every reset path. We have deliberately not
  guessed at a fourth fix.
- So this needs one more pull to catch it. If he misbehaves again, we will
  have the answer in the log this time instead of another guess.

**Under the hood**
- The server now builds from a complete, self-contained copy of its own
  source. A pile of custom content was previously only present on the live
  machine and in no backup - world spawns, table definitions and database
  content that would have been lost outright had that machine died.
- No effect on anything you can see in game. It means a rebuild from scratch
  now produces the realm you are playing on.

**Please test these things**

- Pull Deathbringer Saurfang in Icecrown Citadel and tell us exactly what
  happens - does he still shrug off the pull and go untargetable, and does
  the intro finish before he becomes attackable?
- Alchemists: use Northrend Alchemy Research and check whether the three-day
  cooldown is still being applied.
- Scribes: use Northrend Inscription Research and Minor Inscription Research
  and tell us whether a cooldown still triggers.
- Group up with autoloot on and kill a few things - is loot still being
  skipped, and do emptied corpses still sparkle as though they held something?
- Check whether Emblem of Triumph and similar badges now sync between your
  characters instead of sitting in one character's bags.
- Earn some Stone Keeper's Shards or Wintergrasp Marks of Honor and tell us
  whether the gains look ten times the normal amount.
- Fire mages: does Living Bomb spread on its own yet?
- Affliction warlocks: when your damage-over-time effects spread, do they
  spread out from the infected enemy rather than from you?
