- Elemental shamans' Fire Elemental Totem calls two permanent guardians at 25% size and 2000% extra damage (#167) - the totem's Greater Fire Elemental is summoned through the player, so both it and a twin (summoned directly with the vanilla summon properties, slot 0, so the two coexist) pass the temp-summon stats hook, where Elemental perk owners get them shrunk to quarter scale and their duration zeroed into lives-until-killed; the pair's damage runs through the OnDamage funnel at x21 (+2000 percent, same reading as the Living Bomb and Affliction ladders); a fresh totem despawns the previous pack since slot 0 never replaces anything; non-perk shamans keep the vanilla single 2-minute elemental; no SQL and no core changes

- docs: replace HANDOFF.md handoff with planning-with-files session SOP
- Group autoloot: route bot-kill corpses to the real player, mail bag-full leftovers, and stop junk corpses from sparkling forever (#155) - bot kills swept every corpse into bot bags before the master's autoloot could react, so grouped players saw loot 'missing'; the killer-bot now hands the corpse to the nearest real perk-holder at loot distance (and its auto-skin with it), falling back to its own grab when no real player is in range; PERSONAL_LOOT groups no longer stand autoloot down (normal-mob corpses under personal loot are plain shared looting, and personal-loot bosses were already granted directly at kill); the 5s corpse sweep from #191 never worked because both AnyUnitInObjectRangeCheck and the loop guard required IsAlive() while lootable corpses are in the Corpse state - it now uses a lootable-flag checker and also runs the auto-skin; storage failures are counted as refusals instead of grants (StoreLootItem returns the item pointer even when it failed) and INVENTORY_FULL mails the item to the player with the same BoP trade window the roll path uses, so a full bag no longer leaves a single item sparkling on the corpse; after granting everything possible, corpses whose only leftovers are slots takeable by nobody eligible (quest items for quests nobody has, condition-gated or faction-locked drops) are cleared and despawned on the usual 30s window instead of sparkling forever
