**Bonesaw 0.1.120 - patch notes**

**New**
- Elemental shaman perk: Fire Elemental Totem now calls two permanent, quarter-size Greater Fire Elementals that hit about 20x harder. They stay until something kills them; drop a fresh totem for a new pair.

**Fixes and changes**
- Dungeon and raid trash now drop green items far more often - instance loot tables were much stingier than the open world at the same level.
- Group autoloot reworked: loot from bot kills reaches you instead of vanishing, a full bag mails you the leftover instead of leaving a sparkling corpse, and stuck lootable corpses finally despawn.
- Playerbots can talk: combat taunts, ambient one-liners and crude jokes are now live with proper throttling.
- Daily and weekly quests are infinitely repeatable.
- Account-wide reputation is now team-scoped: one faction's reputations no longer overwrite the other side's, and characters hit by the old bug get repaired.
- Dalaran flight refusals now log server-side diagnostics so any remaining "you can't use that here" can be traced.
