**Bonesaw 0.1.119 - patch notes**

Reputation and currencies
- Reputation is now account-wide: every character's rep gains record for the whole account, online alts update live, and existing standings seed from your best character, so an exalted alt lifts everyone.
- Emblem and badge rewards from random dungeons are up 10x.

Dungeons and bots
- Bots filled into your dungeon now teleport to the entrance every 5 seconds until they arrive, instead of giving up.
- Leaving a group of bots (especially raids) disbands it and returns the bots to the pool.
- The dungeon finder no longer quietly skips the classic-era queues.
- Craft-path addon errors now print the real message to chat instead of a silent popup.

Wintergrasp and siege
- Defender portals teleport you to the fortress and no longer stick a 30s [Teleport] debuff on you.
- Siege vehicles hit 10x harder everywhere, permanently: walls and gates on every map, and the Ulduar vehicle fights.
- Wintergrasp defenders respawn fast and the army is tripled.

Violet Hold
- The visit paces itself for a bot group: one trash portal between each boss, all six prison bosses released in a shuffled order, Cyanigosa at the end, and the roleplay waits are trimmed throughout.

Classes
- Affliction warlock DoTs last until the target dies, and spread keeps re-arming as ranks upgrade.
- Self-cast buffs are permanent and return on resurrection.
- Living Bomb spreads chain-react within one tick instead of one ring per second.

World and client
- Flying mounts work in Dalaran on the server and the client.
- Crafting consumes the reagent bank directly, server-side, account-wide.
- Putricide's insect swarm gauntlet no longer wipes bot-filled raids.
- The armory gets its auto-attune toggle back plus a quality filter.
- The solo-queue toggle was never removed - it lives in the Account Perks window, World tab.

**Please test these things**
- Queue a dungeon and watch the bots - do they arrive on their own in a few seconds?
- Run Violet Hold - do all six prison bosses release, one trash wave between each?
- Drive a siege vehicle into any destructible wall or an Ulduar vehicle fight - is it 10x?
- Craft something that needs only banked materials - does anything land in your bags?
- Gain reputation on one character, then check an alt.
- Use a Wintergrasp defender portal.
- Cast a self-buff, die, and resurrect - is it still there?
- Try a flying mount in Dalaran.

Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.
