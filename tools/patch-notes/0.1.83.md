**Bonesaw 0.1.83 - patch notes**

**Holidays now rotate every 5 days**
- The ten seasonal holidays no longer wait for their real-world date. One is always running, and a new one starts every 5 days.
- Right now it is Children's Week. Next up: Harvest Festival, then Pilgrim's Bounty, then Midsummer, then Brewfest.
- Every holiday comes around within 50 days, so their achievements are ordinary content you can actually finish instead of something you wait a year for.
- The in-game calendar has been updated to show the real rotation dates rather than the old real-world ones.

**Gear growth**
- Fixed items levelling up and gaining nothing but armor. Crit, hit, haste, expertise, attack power, spell power, resilience and armor penetration all grow now.
- Items whose stats come from their name ("of the Bear", "of the Vision", and so on) were growing nothing at all. They now grow properly.
- Weapon damage scales as your weapon levels, so a weapon you like keeps hitting harder instead of falling behind.

**Fixes**
- Fixed a server crash caused by Divine Storm.
- Fixed Holy Shock being able to cause the same crash.

**Perks panel**
- Rows are taller and descriptions have room to breathe, so longer perks no longer overlap the row below.
- Tracks you earn by playing now show simple progress (like 4 / 10) instead of a row of squares you cannot click.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
