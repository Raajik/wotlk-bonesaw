**Bonesaw 0.1.103 - patch notes**

Seven specs got full perk reworks - real kits instead of flat damage
buffs:

Warlock Demonology - The Imp Lord:
- Fel Domination is now Imp Legion: summon 8 imps that fight alongside
  you. Recast to refresh, recast while they are out to dismiss them.
- Metamorphosis no longer costs mana or cooldown, and while it lasts
  your imp legion becomes felguards.
- Your demon pet and the whole legion deal doubled damage.

Warrior Protection - Lord of Shields and Thunder:
- Shield Slam hits 4 times, and each hit ruptures everything within
  8 yards.
- Thunder Clap has triple the radius and applies Rend and Deep Wounds
  at triple damage.
- New ability Avalanche: place a 12-yard circle of falling rock that
  damages and slows everything inside. Cast again to move it, cast it
  on the circle to dismiss. You block 25% more while it is on the
  ground.

Priest Shadow - The Void Choir:
- Every Mind Flay summons 2 shadow constructs that fight with you
  (up to 6).
- Mind Blast detonates Shadow Word: Pain - deals all its remaining
  damage at once, then reapplies it at full duration.
- Voidform (recast Shadowform to toggle): a free Mind Blast fires
  itself every 3 seconds.

Paladin Retribution - Sanctified Whirlwind:
- Learn Sanctified Whirlwind, a toggle: your Consecration follows you
  as you move, and Divine Storm's cooldown clears whenever you deal
  Holy damage.
- While the Divine Storm window lasts, your melee swings also strike
  everything within 8 yards for half damage.
- Crusader Strike has a 30% chance to fire a free Avenger's Shield
  bounce chain.

Hunter Survival - The Trap Engineer:
- Traps left on the ground re-arm themselves every 10 seconds (up to
  3 times) while an enemy stands in them.
- Explosive Shot stacks Shrapnel on the target - at 5 stacks the next
  hit detonates all of it in an explosion.
- New ability Call of the Wilds: summon 2 taunt bears that grab and
  hold a pack while you shoot.

Shaman Enhancement - The Storm Caller:
- Stormstrike marks its target Charged. Lightning Bolt or Chain
  Lightning on a Charged target fires a second bolt at half damage.
- Static Field (toggle via Thorns): a storm follows you, striking
  everything nearby with damage that scales with your attack power.
- Your spirit wolves' strikes have a 25% chance to call down lightning.

Death Knight Unholy:
- A permanent blight shroud surrounds you: every 2 seconds it applies
  (or refreshes) Blood Plague, Frost Fever and Ebon Plague on all
  enemies within 10 yards, and your diseases deal 5x damage.
- Army of the Dead lasts as long as you stay in combat.
- Your Gargoyle stays until killed.

All tank specs (Protection Warrior and Paladin, Feral Druid in Bear
Form, Blood Death Knight) now generate 10x threat.
