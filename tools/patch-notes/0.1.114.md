**Bonesaw 0.1.114 - patch notes**

**Changes**
- Blessing of Freedom perk is now a self-only toggle on the casting paladin - recast it to turn it off - and it moves you 50% instead of 30%
- The DoTs that spread from a warlock's Haunt now jump up to 40 yards between enemies (was 15)
- Anub'arak no longer burrows at 75/50/25% health - the fight is continuous now. Heads up: he is easier than before, since the extra adds that only spawned while he was underground no longer appear
- The /report form: Enter makes a new line and Ctrl+Enter sends, shift+click items in your bags to link them, and a live counter shows how much room is left
- Craft-all from the reagent bank: if a craft is refused, the diag line now names the recipe you actually clicked, not a neighbor
