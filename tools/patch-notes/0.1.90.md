**Bonesaw 0.1.90 - patch notes**

* Rogue
  - Pickpocket now works like a proper crowd skill: using it pickpockets every
    humanoid within 10 yards at once.
  - Shadowstep still pickpockets everything within 20 yards when you land.

* Warrior (Arms)
  - Bladestorm now lasts forever while active -- recast it to stop. Same free
    cast, no rage cost, and it still auto-fires Whirlwind and Thunder Clap
    while you spin.
