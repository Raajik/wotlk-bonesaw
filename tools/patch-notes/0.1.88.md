**Bonesaw 0.1.88 - patch notes**

* Rogue perks
  - Hemorrhage spread now only throws Ambush and Garrote at enemies you could actually hit: in melee range and in front of you.
  - Ambush spread no longer requires a dagger -- it works from whatever weapon you swing.
  - Bleed effects from the spread no longer fail on undead, mechanical, or elemental enemies; they are skipped cleanly.

* Warlock
  - Added behind-the-scenes tracking to the Affliction DoT-spread perk so any future issue comes with real evidence.
