**Bonesaw 0.1.102 - patch notes**

Warlock Affliction:
- Haunt now teaches Unstable Affliction and applies it, plus Corruption,
  Curse of Agony AND Curse of the Elements (both curses now stack).
- Haunt can be cast while moving.
- Affliction damage-over-time effects now deal 2000% more damage.

Druid Balance:
- Starfall hits have a 30% chance to fire a free Moonfire at the target,
  which brings its own standalone Hurricane.
- Insect Swarm now spreads on its own from enemy to enemy, and refreshes
  on targets that already have it.

Mage Fire:
- Living Bomb now jumps from enemy to enemy on its own, like the other
  spreading effects.

Loot:
- Personal loot reworked: every group member loots the full drop table
  independently. What one person takes does not disappear for others -
  no rolls and no fighting over corpses. Skinning stays one-taker.

Dungeon finder:
- Groups now fill as 1 tank, 1 healer, 3 dps instead of 5 dps.
- Classic and lower-level dungeons are visible again when queueing with
  lower-level friends.

Rogue:
- Lockpicking is account-wide again: any of your characters can
  auto-unlock chests and open lockboxes in bags.

Party XP:
- Fixed an over-generous party XP bonus: bots were inflating everyone's
  kill XP. Bonuses now come from the highest human in the party.

Attunement:
- Items whose stats come from a random suffix (like Tumultuous
  Necklaces) now attune correctly and bank their secondary stats.

Tip: still seeing "Binds when picked up" on gear that should not? Your
client's item cache is stale - delete your Wow Cache folder, or run
Bonesaw.exe and it will clear it on the next update.
