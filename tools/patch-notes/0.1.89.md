**Bonesaw 0.1.89 - patch notes**

* Druid (Balance)
  - Insect Swarm spreads to every enemy within 25 yards when you cast it, and
    auto-casts on whatever hits you in combat.
  - Moonkin Form is granted.
  - Thorns covers your whole party or raid, at +2000% damage.
  - Starfall now keeps dealing damage forever while toggled on, not just for
    the first ten seconds.

* Haste
  - All damage-over-time and heal-over-time effects now tick faster as your
    spell haste improves, without lasting any shorter. Better gear, faster
    dots and hots.

* Rogue
  - While stealthed, you automatically pickpocket every humanoid within 25
    yards (once per stealth).
  - Locked chests now auto-unlock and auto-loot themselves when your
    lockpicking is high enough.
  - Shadow Dance is a real spell in your spellbook now, on top of the party
    attack-power buff.

* Paladin (Protection)
  - Judgement has a chance to fire a full Avenger's Shield bounce chain for
    free.

* Warrior (Protection)
  - You generate 11x threat. Holding aggro is no longer a fight.

* Professions
  - Skill-up perks now apply to every profession, including First Aid and the
    gathering professions, and whichever profession you level first speeds up
    all the rest.

* Account-wide
  - Emblems (Triumph, Frost, etc) now go straight into the shared account
    vault when you loot them.

* Housekeeping
  - Removed the dead *Attune Backpack button (its mechanic was retired; the
    Items panel is where attuned gear lives).
  - City NPCs like Vern in Dalaran are no longer marked as rare.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
