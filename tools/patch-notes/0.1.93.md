**Bonesaw 0.1.93 - patch notes**

**Class perks**
- Affliction damage-over-time effects now spread from your selected enemy without requiring an auto-attack target.

**Account perks**
- Retired and passive perk entries are cleaned out of character spellbooks without removing account ownership.
- Ore, herb, and fishing-pool tracking no longer overrides your chosen minimap tracking mode.

**Dungeon Finder**
- Classic and Burning Crusade normal and heroic dungeons remain available at level 80.
- Random Classic, Burning Crusade, and Burning Crusade Heroic queues remain available at level 80.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
