**Bonesaw 0.1.99 - patch notes**

Crafting and the reagent bank
- Crafting now pays for materials straight out of the reagent bank. Nothing is pulled into your bags any more, not even for a moment.
- Tools and crafting materials stay in the bank where you left them.
- Profession windows count everything the bank holds, so the craftable totals are honest again.
- The only way an item leaves the reagent bank is the Take button. No more silent withdrawing behind the scenes.

Attunement
- Attuning gear now banks every secondary stat: defense, dodge, parry, block value, mp5, health regen and spell penetration, not just the basics. Tank and healer gear attunes whole.
- Attack power items share their ranged attack power too.
- Fixed collection attunement failing to save for some items.

Paladin
- Avenger's Shield no longer gets stuck on a cooldown that lasts for hours. Casting it feels normal again.

Misc
- Research spells no longer have a cooldown.
