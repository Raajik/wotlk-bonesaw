**Bonesaw 0.1.104 - patch notes**

**Playerbots**
- Bots no longer open trade with you unprompted - this time including the companion (addclass) bots that kept slipping through
- Bots filled into your dungeon group now arrive at the dungeon entrance instead of wherever they were idling - no more typing "summon" five times

**Crafting**
- The reagent bank finally behaves: batch-crafting out of it no longer hits phantom "missing reagent" errors, and the profession window's craftable counts refresh the moment materials are consumed instead of going stale

**Paladin (Protection)**
- Avenger's Shield is back on a 6-second cooldown
- Judgement and Hammer of the Righteous each have a 50% chance to fire a free Avenger's Shield that bounces through up to 30 enemies

**World**
- The Shadowfang Keep courtyard door is gone - walk straight in

**Warlock (Affliction)** [shipped 0.1.102/103, now live on all clients]
- Haunt is instant and castable while moving, applies Unstable Affliction plus your curses, and no longer eats its own cooldown
