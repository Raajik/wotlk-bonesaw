**Bonesaw 0.1.134 - patch notes**

**Changes**
- The realm has moved to a new machine. Run the launcher once and it points your client at the new address for you - nothing to edit by hand. If you connect manually instead, the login address is now 100.89.249.40.
- Borean Tundra: fixed the swarm of Nerub'ar Sky Darkeners piling onto the Warsong Captain outside Warsong Hold. They were being replaced faster than anything could kill them and never despawned on their own, so hundreds stacked up on a captain who could not die. The assault ends the way it should now.
- That pile-up was quietly eating a large slice of the server's time every hour, so the world should feel a little smoother generally.
