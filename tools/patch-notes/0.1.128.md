**Bonesaw 0.1.128 - patch notes**

**Cooldown and crafting fixes**
- The Avenger's Shield button now shows what the server is actually doing: a real 6-second sweep instead of flashing 30 seconds and then going blank, and it no longer randomly locks itself for 30 seconds again mid-fight.
- Smelting now gets the profession skill-up boost like every other craft - your perk multiplier applies to Smelt Thorium and the rest of Mining's recipes, not just to the bench professions.
- Craft batches from the reagent bank stop the moment you move or jump - no more crafting that keeps going until you relog.

**Please test these things**
- Run a Create All batch from the reagent bank, then walk away or jump halfway through - crafting should stop immediately.
- Smelt some thorium - the skill-ups should come in boosted like your other crafts do.
- Paladins: cast Avenger's Shield by hand and also trigger a free one off Judgement or Hammer of the Righteous - the button should show a 6-second sweep, unlock at 6 seconds, and never flash 30 again.
- Northrend Alchemy and Inscription research - are their cooldowns still showing 3 days / 20 hours?
- Group up and check autoloot still picks everything up with the personal loot rules on.
- Check whether Emblem of Triumph (and similar currencies) are syncing across your characters yet.
- Fire mages: does Living Bomb spread between enemies on its own?

This update is server-side only - you do not need to update your client.
