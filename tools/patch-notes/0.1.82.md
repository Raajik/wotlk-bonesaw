**Bonesaw 0.1.82 - patch notes**

**Perks panel**
- Split into two tabs on the achievement window: **Unlocks** and **Perks**.
- **Unlocks** holds the one-off conveniences - mailbox, bank, autoloot, riding, tracking and the rest. Each one shows its icon, what it does, and what it costs, on one line. Click a row you can afford.
- **Perks** holds the things that come in ranks. Each row now tells you what the next rank does and what it costs, without needing to hover anything.
- Perk icons now match what the perk actually does. Mailbox was a warrior stomp, Bank was Sap, Autoloot was a fireball. 94 of them were wrong.

**Movement**
- Fixed Wayfarer ranks arriving out of order. If you had earned an exploration rank you could end up owning a later rank without the ones before it, and buying those lower ranks would have given you no speed at all.
- Earning a Wayfarer rank now grants every rank below it too.

**Launcher**
- The launcher now clears your cached item and spell data whenever it installs new client files. Without it, changes like the new icons would keep showing the old version until you cleared it by hand.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
