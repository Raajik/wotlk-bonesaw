Title: Bonesaw 0.1.115 - patch notes

Bugs
- Affliction warlocks: your spreading DoTs now visibly creep from infected
  monsters to new victims instead of streaming out of you (report #156).

  repaired from the stored full text.

Content
- Flying mounts now work in Dalaran; the anti-flight debuff no longer applies
  there. Wintergrasp's flight restriction is unchanged.
- All doors have been removed from Dire Maul - wings, Gordok gates, the
  conservatory door, the crumble wall and the pylon force fields.
- Archmage Lan'dalock's weekly raid quests now award 250 Emblems of Frost and
  250 Emblems of Triumph per turn-in (was 5 of each).
- PvP objectives across the world capture about 25x faster: Wintergrasp
  banners, Halaa, the Hellfire / Eastern Plaguelands / Terokkar / Zangarmarsh
  towers, Venture Bay lighthouse -- and Eye of the Storm's towers too.
