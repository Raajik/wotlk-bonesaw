**Bonesaw 0.1.116 - patch notes**

**Reports & bug tracker**
- The report window is much larger with a taller text area, so nothing gets cut off behind the Send button.
- The 60-second wait between reports from the same account is gone - file as many as you need. Length caps still apply.
- Item links in reports now arrive as [item:ID], so shift+clicked items make it through in one piece.

**Spells**
- Fixed: Lightning Bolt hit for billions because the spell data behind Chain Lightning was corrupted. Bolts hit for sane amounts again.
- Living Bomb now spreads to enemies within 40 yards (was 15) and hits far harder, matching the Affliction DoT ladder.
- Fixed: Avenger's Shield left a stale 30 second cooldown on the button after casting. The real 6 second cooldown now shows.

**Perks**
- Reputation perks now also multiply reputation from items and tokens, like the Zandalari Honor Token.

**PvP**
- Wintergrasp armies are larger: 5 spawns per faction row, up from 3, for both Alliance and Horde.

**Please test these things**

- Paladins: cast Avenger's Shield - does the button show the 6 second cooldown instead of a stale 30?
- Shamans: throw Chain Lightning - is the damage back to sane numbers instead of billions?
- File two reports back to back - no more 60 second wait, and all your text stays visible above the Send button.
