**Bonesaw 0.1.117 - patch notes**

**Launcher**
- Full retheme: charcoal and blood, with a new BS monogram mark in Uncial Antiqua over a blood sawtooth underline
- New auto-login setup button: save your account and password (encrypted, stays on your machine) and the launcher types them at the login screen
- Auto-login reads the account name saved in your client and only types once the game window is verified front and center, so nothing goes to the wrong window
- The auto-login window pauses the launch until you close it
- Wow now launches in the foreground, ready for input
- Launch countdown ("starting in N") so quick updates do not blink past
- The launcher refreshes the Windows icon cache after self-updates, so the taskbar icon stays current

**Crafting**
- Crafts that need more reagents than your bags hold now pull the shortfall from your reagent vault automatically and finish the craft

**Please test these things**
- Set up auto-login from the launcher and launch Wow - does it come to the front and log you in?
- Craft something whose reagents are in your vault but not your bags - does it finish?
- Next time the launcher updates itself, does the taskbar icon show the new BS mark?
- Craft as a Blacksmith or Leatherworker with speed perks - do the speeds apply?
- Use Living Bomb in combat - does it spread on its own?
- Pick a lockable chest as a rogue - does it unlock and autoloot?
- Try Avenger's Shield - is the cooldown shorter, or does it proc off other abilities?
- Tank as a protection warrior - does aggro hold?

Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.
