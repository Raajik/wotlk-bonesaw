**Bonesaw 0.1.127 - patch notes**

**Boss fixes**
- XT-002, Mimiron's Aerial Command Unit, and Gormok's Snobold rider were still hitting like siege engines - far harder than what your combat log showed. All three are back to normal boss damage.
- The Runic Colossus at the end of Thorim's gauntlet now fights you when you reach it.
- Deathbringer Saurfang can no longer be pulled into a broken start: even if his walk to the platform is slowed to a crawl, he now stops, takes his place, the door closes, and only then does the fight begin.

**Class fixes**
- Avenger's Shield frees up properly again: when a Judgement or Hammer of the Righteous triggers a free shield, the button comes back 6 seconds later instead of staying locked. Hand-casting it is clean too.
- Cloak of Shadows no longer sticks around forever after you die and recast it.

**Crafting, dungeons and flavor**
- Crafting a slow recipe straight from the reagent bank now completes the whole batch instead of resetting the cast bar halfway through and throwing away the rest.
- Using an ICC teleport pad no longer leaves a permanent teleport buff sitting on your bar.
- Your bot groupmates picked up a whole new batch of terrible things to say about your gear. You'll know it when you hear it.

**Please test these things**
- Craft a slow recipe from the reagent bank (Runecloth Bandages are a good one) - does the whole batch finish without the cast bar resetting halfway?
- Paladins: fire a free Avenger's Shield off a Judgement or Hammer of the Righteous - is the button usable again 6 seconds later? Cast it by hand too and check the same.
- Take a swing at XT-002 in Ulduar and at Gormok with his Snobold rider in the Argent Coliseum - do they hit for roughly what your combat log shows?
- Walk down Thorim's gauntlet - the Runic Colossus at the end should now engage. While you're in ICC, hop on a teleport pad and check that the teleport buff disappears from your bar.
- Pull Deathbringer Saurfang a couple of times - he should be fighting you on the platform with the door shut.
- Rogues: is lockpicking showing up on your character (auto-unlocking chests and lockboxes)?
- Level in a party and see whether XP still feels inflated compared to soloing.
- Northrend Alchemy and Inscription research - are their research cooldowns still showing 3 days / 20 hours?

This update is server-side only - you do not need to update your client.
