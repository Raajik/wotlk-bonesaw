**Bonesaw 0.1.130 - patch notes**

**Fixes**
- Fixed a server crash that took the realm down whenever certain characters logged in. The realm was restarting in a loop because the bad state was never cleared, so each restart walked straight back into it. Cleaned up and closed off.
- Chimera Shot works again. If your Serpent Sting was one of the permanent ones it was quietly paying out nothing at all; it now deals its full damage, and the Viper Sting mana return was fixed the same way.
- Lifeblood no longer rolls a nonsense healing number. Herbalists were getting a garbage amount rather than the heal the buff advertises.

**Connecting**
- The realm now hands out its Tailscale name as the address. Nothing changes if you play on the same machine.

- Hunters: put Serpent Sting on a target and fire Chimera Shot - does it hit for a solid chunk instead of nothing?
- Herbalists: use Lifeblood and watch the heal - does it restore a sensible amount rather than something absurd?
