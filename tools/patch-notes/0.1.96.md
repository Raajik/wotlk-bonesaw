**Bonesaw 0.1.96 - patch notes**

**Attuned items**
- Attuned gear shows its real name again. Every entry in the list was called "Item".
- Entries your client has never seen now have a tooltip showing the name, item level and stats instead of nothing at all.
- The list scrolls. Only the first handful of entries were reachable before, no matter how many you had attuned, and there was nothing on screen saying otherwise. Use the mouse wheel.
- Attune All moved to the bottom of the panel, and the list shows more rows.

**Class tab**
- Spec cards fill the panel instead of a fixed box, so longer specs no longer spill their text outside the card.
- Fixed a doubled bullet on sub-perk lines.

*Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.*
