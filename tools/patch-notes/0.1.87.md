**Bonesaw 0.1.87 - patch notes**

Combat
- Mobs scaled to your level now fight at that level for real: hits, spells, dodges and crits all roll against the level you see on their frame. Low-level characters in scaled zones should land attacks normally again.

Account-wide
- Achievements are now truly account-wide: anything earned before the sync existed is backfilled to every character at login.

Quality of life
- The Quests - Finish perk summons each turn-in NPC once, instead of one copy per completed quest.
- Track Ore and Track Herbs no longer reset your own minimap tracking choice every few seconds.
