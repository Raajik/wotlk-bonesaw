**Bonesaw 0.1.86 - patch notes**

Bug fixes
- Playerbots no longer start trades with you. They will still complete a trade you open.
- Lockboxes and junkboxes in your bags can now be sent to the reagent vault by hand, matching what auto-loot already did. Taking one back out still picks the lock for you.
- The Account Perks window no longer pops open on its own while you fight.

Close Wow and run Bonesaw.exe to update.
