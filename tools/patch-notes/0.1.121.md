**Bonesaw 0.1.121 - patch notes**

**Changes**

*Progression*
- The quest log now colors quests at YOUR level instead of their authored level. Quests read yellow like the mobs around them, matching how the world already scales creatures and pays reward XP - no more gray quests that still fight back.

*PvP - Wintergrasp*
- Playerbots now join Wintergrasp wars that real players are fighting. Each side fills with bots scaled to how many real players are engaged - 3 bots per real player by default, up to 15 per side - landing at their faction's home graveyard and fighting through the battle. A war with no real players in it stays bot-free.
