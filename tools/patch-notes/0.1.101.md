**Bonesaw 0.1.101 - patch notes**

Warlock Affliction:
- Haunt is learned by the perk and casts instantly.
- Haunt automatically applies Unstable Affliction, Corruption, Curse of
  Agony and Curse of the Elements to the target; those curses then spread
  with your other DoTs.
- All your Affliction damage-over-time effects deal 4x damage.

Druid Balance:
- Moonfire automatically applies a standalone Hurricane to the target.
- Wrath also fires a free instant Starfire at the same target, and
  Starfire fires a free instant Wrath back.

Grouping:
- Everyone in a party now kills with the same XP bonus - the highest one
  in the party - and grouping with lower-level players no longer reduces
  your kill XP. Quest XP is unchanged.

Loot:
- All loot is personal. In a group there are no more rolls, no master
  looter and no round robin - everything that drops can be looted by any
  member, first come first served. Gold still splits.

Characters:
- Up to 20 characters per realm. (The character select screen still shows
  10 slots at a time; a fuller screen rework is coming separately.)

Fixes:
- Fixed a crash when items were attuned while the perks window was open.
